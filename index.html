Setup

Installed in three minutes

  1. 1

    Download and open the DMG

    Double-click the downloaded file to open the PhoneHub Pro installer window.

  2. 2

    Run “INSTALL PHONEHUB PRO”

    It copies the app to Applications and prepares the built-in engine. The first time, macOS may say it is from an unidentified developer — that is normal for independent apps. Right-click the installer → Open → Open. Ten seconds, once only, and never again.

    Open PhoneHub Pro and add your first phone

Questions

Before you install

Is my shop data sent anywhere?

No. Inventory, bills and repairs are stored in a single database on your Mac, with automatic daily backups. The optional AI assistant is turned off until you connect it, and can run entirely on your own machine.

Will macOS or Windows warn me when I open the app?

Once — and it is a 10-second fix. PhoneHub Pro is distributed directly by its maker instead of through paid developer programs, so a new computer shows a warning the first time. On Mac: right-click (or Control-click) the installer → Open → Open. On Windows: click More info → Run anyway on the blue SmartScreen screen. The installed app opens normally after that, forever.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. The download section has a separate Windows 10/11 installer (64-bit). It carries the same shop features and also runs its engine locally — your data stays on your own computer.

Do I need a subscription?

No. There is nothing to buy and no monthly fee. Optional cloud AI keys are yours and are only used if you turn the assistant on.

Can two Macs share the same shop?

Each Mac keeps its own local records. PhoneHub Pro includes a local sync guide for shops that want two counters to work together.

Need help?

Email support@phonehubpro.com with your Mac model and what happened. A screenshot of the screen is the fastest way to get an answer.
