Questions

Before you install

Is my shop data sent anywhere?

No. Inventory, bills and repairs are stored in a single database on your computer, with automatic daily backups. Nothing to sign up for, and it opens without internet.

Will macOS or Windows warn me when I open the app?

Once — and it is a 10-second fix. PhoneHub Pro is distributed directly by its maker instead of through paid developer programs, so a new computer shows a warning the first time. On Mac: right-click (or Control-click) the installer → Open → Open. On Windows: click More info → Run anyway. The installed app opens normally after that.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. The same shop features, a separate Windows 10/11 installer (64-bit), also running entirely on your own computer.

Do I need a subscription?

No. There is nothing to buy and no monthly fee.

How does the website know where I am?

From your connection, not your device — there is no location prompt. Only the city and country are used to look up the current weather, and nothing is stored on our side.

Does the sky need internet?

The live sky on this page does, and so does the app's Sky board — it keeps the last sky it saw for offline use. The shop itself never needs the internet.

Need a hand?

Email support@phonehubpro.com with your computer model and what happened. A screenshot gets you the fastest answer.
